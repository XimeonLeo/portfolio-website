Why n8n?

It handles high volume and AI-heavy work without per-task fees adding up, it can run on your own server if your data has to stay in-house, and when a step needs real code, it has a code node instead of a dead end. If your team already lives in GoHighLevel, I build there and connect it to n8n for the heavy lifting.

Is it safe to let AI read our documents?

AI only does the reading and sorting. Anything that has to be exact is checked by code before it's written anywhere, and anything uncertain is flagged for a person to approve. Sensitive fields like SSNs can be left out of the AI step entirely.

Can you fix an automation someone else built?

Yes. I start by working out what the workflow actually processed versus what it should have, explain what's broken in plain English, then fix and document it.

How does a project start?

With a free 30-minute process audit. You walk me through the task; I come back with a written scope: what happens today, what the system will do, a fixed price or milestone plan, and an honest estimate of how likely each part is to work.

How long does a build take?

A single workflow, fix or chatbot usually takes 3 to 10 days. A full pipeline like the closing-document system takes 2 to 4 weeks, delivered in working pieces so you see progress every few days.

What happens after handover?

You own everything and get a guide your team can follow. If you want me on call, there's a monthly retainer for monitoring, fixes and new automations as you grow.

Are you an AI automation agency or a freelancer?

I'm a freelance AI automation specialist. You get the systems an AI automation agency would build, but you work directly with the person building them, and everything runs on your own accounts.

Do you build AI voice agents and WhatsApp AI chatbots?
